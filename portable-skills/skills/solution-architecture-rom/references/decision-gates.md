# Selective Go/NoGo, paid discovery and re-estimation

## Gate the consequence, not the entire programme

Each gate record: `id`, `decision or affected capability`, `why it matters`, `status`, `blocked_wps`, `unblocked_wps`, `evidence_to_unblock`, `decision_owner`, `next_check`, `if_unresolved`, `reversibility`.

Example fictional case: Unknown source timestamp semantics blocks automated OEE attribution and the equipment-event integration estimate. It need not block an independently operated manual checklist pilot. Unknown quality release rights blocks *automatic release* but may still permit supervised observation/prototyping **subject to actual authorisation**.

## Suggested decision sequence

G0 **Problem/authority agreement:** Is the capability worth addressing, and can decision roles be named?

G1 **Discovery and evidence:** Which unknown dominates the choice or estimate? Paid discovery should have short, measurable deliverables and decisions, not “more workshops.”

G2 **Option and MVP:** Compare the no-build, configure/integrate and new-product patterns. Decide what is trialled and what is excluded.

G3 **Design and responsibility:** Authorise interfaces, state meanings, operational roles, security boundaries, technical ownership and acceptance.

G4 **Commercial:** Independently review effort, licences, schedule dependencies, support and explicit unestimated WPs. This skill cannot grant financial approval.

G5 **Release:** Only after validation, operational acceptance and appropriate safety/quality/security controls; never issued by this skill.

## Stop / defer / proceed conditions

- `PROCEED FOR DESIGN REVIEW`: evidence adequate for scoped engineering design only.
- `PROCEED FOR BOUNDED PILOT REVIEW`: reversible and approved pilot that avoids the blocked high-consequence pathways.
- `PAID DISCOVERY`: decisive unknowns can be answered through short authorised tests.
- `DEFER`: no owner, no authority, or insufficient prospective value/evidence.
- `STOP`: unsafe or unauthorised request, material unresolved safety/regulatory requirement, or confidentiality violation. Escalate to qualified humans.

No decision label here is a binding approval. Actual sign-off must cite an external record.
